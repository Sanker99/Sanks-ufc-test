Data transparency

Game logs are sourced from nflverse weekly player statistics. Selected game logs can be cross-checked against ESPN final box scores. Current and historical injury listings are published by scheduled cloud jobs; the dashboard checks for published changes without page reloads. Injury listings are not independently verified by the NFL. Not listed does not mean healthy. Confirmed OUT injury absences are shown as 0 DNP; unconfirmed absences and early exits are not inferred.
